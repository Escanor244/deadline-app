Kairos is a countdown lock screen studio and reverse-engineering deadline planner. It helps users work backward from target deadlines to generate actionable milestone schedules, calculate daily pacing, and design high-resolution custom lock screen wallpapers for iPhone, Android, and desktop displays. The application features zero-backend offline persistence, standard calendar sync with alarm alerts, live fullscreen HUD mode, and a universal AI planning engine compatible with Groq, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, and OpenAI.
